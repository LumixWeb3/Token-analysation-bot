# Market phases, audience and selective setups

This is a research lens, not a score, compulsory output form or automatic trading rule. It supplements the system policy. Use another explanation when it better fits the evidence. Describe the relevant sector's phase in the living strategy, including observations for and against it. Do not force the entire market or every coin into the same phase.

## Separate three different clocks

Sector phase describes attention and liquidity across related exact mints. Token lifecycle describes one coin's discovery, growth, consolidation, decline or reactivation. Audience discovery describes who already knows and which different audience could arrive next. They can disagree: a fresh coin may launch into a fatigued sector; an older leader may benefit from a genuinely new audience.

Being early to an event, early to price and early to a new audience are different. A known future catalyst can already be priced in. A large public story does not establish demand for this exact mint. Explain the conversion: who encounters the story, through which channel, why they would identify and choose this token, and what present evidence supports that possibility.

## Explicit sector phases

**Rotation / ignition.** Attention and liquidity may be moving into an emerging theme. Look for a credible new catalyst, sustained relative strength, participation across independent wallets and related tokens, and an emerging leader. Compare the prior sector as well. One first pump or one famous wallet does not establish leadership. Investigate the strongest candidate promptly; do not spend its whole opportunity researching unrelated low-information tokens.

**Euphoria / expansion.** Attention broadens; leaders, followers and copycats proliferate. Distinguish real transmission of demand from liquidity dilution. An established leader may remain the best vehicle; a smaller derivative is not entitled to catch up. A larger-cap exception still needs incremental buyers, an available setup and sensible remaining opportunity. Popularity alone does not establish an edge; euphoria can also be distribution into new buyers.

**Fatigue.** Breadth weakens: marginal launches fail to follow through, fewer survivors absorb volume, new participation decays, breakouts fail or catalysts stop producing sustained response. Seek sequential, sector-wide evidence rather than declaring fatigue from one dip. Prefer waiting or narrowly supported survivor setups over mechanically buying a discount. Reassess whether an existing holding still has its expected source of future demand.

**Cooked / exhaustion.** Broad collapse or inactivity accompanies liquidity leaving the theme. Cash and research elsewhere may be the right response. A fresh independent catalyst can support a reactivation hypothesis, but old ATHs and nostalgia do not prove a new rotation. Require new evidence before treating an old narrative as reborn.

Use **uncertain**, **mixed** or **transition** when appropriate. Different audiences and related tokens can be in different stages. Phase confidence comes from evidence breadth, persistence and contrary observations, not a required numerical percentage. The labels are descriptive hypotheses, not a guaranteed cycle or verified verbatim quotation by James Wang.

## Be incredibly selective without inventing certainty

For a promising candidate, explain the observable edge: what matters, why it is not already fully recognised, which exact mint captures it, what achievable setup makes timing attractive, and what would invalidate the thesis. Compare the leader, plausible alternatives and doing nothing. An impressive story with no identifiable timing or conversion advantage may deserve a watch rather than entry. If there is no visible supported edge in the checked market, do not trade. Distinguish that judgement from required data being unavailable.

Research can resolve present facts; it cannot prove a future return. Do not require guaranteed adoption, but do not rename an untested story an edge. Record what specific observation would justify upgrading a watch and which one would cancel it. Watch conditions must be capable of firing before the intended opportunity, not only after a move has happened.

Use fresh Migrated observations to notice emerging leaders and qualified Trending 24h observations to compare sustained participation. The default Trending maximum token age is seven days; that age filter is separate from its 24-hour ranking window. Both are discovery sources. Filters narrow the observed sample, so absence from a filtered list is not absence of activity. Older held positions and retained watches remain monitored independently of discovery filters.

## Wallet evidence and the top twenty

Read the exact-token top-20 holder projection only with its order, coverage, query and collection proof. Preserve all returned records and provider labels. A GMGN KOL tag means the provider labels that wallet; it does not independently prove identity, a recent purchase, conviction or future returns. Missing tags mean unknown, not proven absence of KOLs. A tagged wallet outside a partial top-20 result does not become absent from the token.

Where accessible, inspect current retained quantity, buy versus transfer, timing, changes and exits. Shared pools, exchanges, routers and bridges can connect unrelated holders. Do not infer common ownership from an infrastructure hub or add overlapping insider/bundler/holder percentages. Wallet history and concentration support interpretation; neither a celebrity label nor historical wallet PnL replaces an exact-token demand thesis. Do not invent unavailable forensic tools or restore V1's obsolete primary bundle/security scores.

## Mainstream pickup and thesis management

Distinguish the original event, crypto attention, actual independent wider coverage and conversion into participation in this mint. A story reaching mainstream media may be the next demand channel, already-priced news or a sell-the-news event. Test which is occurring; do not enforce an automatic media-stage buy or sell. Official ownership and voluntary endorsement require recipient-side or official evidence, not a reused brand, linked handle or unsolicited fee payment.

Review attention together with participant breadth, liquidity, relative strength and the remaining catalyst. Quiet posting alone is not an exit rule; rising attention can coexist with distribution. Trims, holds, additions and exits must remain tied to the current thesis, real position/account state and achievable quotes. A 7.5x ambition does not require waiting for 7.5x or ignoring a sensible exit.

After outcomes, preserve original expectations and costs. A win may be luck; a loss may be variance. Update the strategy only to the extent new evidence supports. A later collapse does not erase an earlier opportunity, and an intraminute peak does not prove a feasible trade.

## Reference status

Sector labels are descriptive hypotheses. Revalidate current market and source facts for every thesis.
